Privacy, cookies and accessibility.

What this site processes, what it does not track, and the accessibility measures currently in place.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

01 / Privacy & cookie

A deliberately low-data website.

Controller and contact

The data controller is Paolo De Marinis. For privacy requests: paolodemarinis1993@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

This website has no account, contact form or newsletter. The source repository is private; pages and documents published with GitHub Pages are public. GitHub may process technical connection data and logs to deliver, protect and diagnose the service under its own notices. The site owner does not use that data for analytics or profiling. For any processing controlled directly by the site owner, the legal basis is the legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure.

If you choose the email link, your email application—not this website—sends the message. The information you voluntarily provide is used only to answer you or to take steps at your request. The legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps at your request or, for other messages, the legitimate interest in replying. It is kept only as long as needed for that exchange and any applicable legal obligations.

Cookies, local storage and tracking

The site does not directly use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies and does not embed third-party media. It stores only the light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage; the value remains on your device and can be removed through browser settings. No consent banner is shown because the current site does not activate non-essential tracking.

Recipients, external links and transfers

The site is published through GitHub Pages from a private source repository. Pages and documents distributed by Pages are publicly accessible. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical data and logs under their own notices and retention rules. The site owner does not sell data collected by the site. GitHub, LinkedIn, RIVES, DOI and other external sites act under their own privacy notices when you choose to visit them.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority. Use the controller email above to exercise your rights.

Official references: GDPR, Article 13 · Italian DPA cookie FAQ · GitHub General Privacy Statement

02 /

Designed toward WCAG 2.2 AA.

Measures in place

The site uses semantic landmarks and headings, a skip link, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, sufficient text contrast, responsive layouts, language metadata, light and dark themes, and reduced-motion support. Content and functions do not depend on color alone.

Known limitations

Some linked academic PDFs may not contain complete tagging for assistive technologies. Third-party websites are outside this site’s control. If a document or passage is difficult to use, request an accessible alternative by email.

Feedback

Report an accessibility problem to paolodemarinis1993@gmail.com. Please include the page, the problem and the assistive technology or browser used, when relevant.

This is a voluntary accessibility note, not an AgID declaration or a formal certification of conformance.

Technical reference: WCAG 2.2
